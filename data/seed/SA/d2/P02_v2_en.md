# Opening an individual savings account - Version 2
Effective date: 2026-09-01

This version supersedes version 1.

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this procedure is to outline the standardized process for opening an individual savings account, ensuring compliance with bank regulations and providing clear guidance for both branch staff and digital channels.

## 2. Scope
This procedure applies to all bank employees handling account openings and covers both in-branch applications and digital applications via the mobile application.

## 3. Steps
1. **Application Submission**: The customer may initiate the account opening process through two primary channels:
    * **Branch Visit**: The customer visits a physical branch to complete the application with a relationship officer.
    * **Mobile Application**: The customer can open the account directly via the mobile application. This new channel allows for account opening without the need to visit a branch, utilizing the "Nafath" platform for identity verification.
2. **Identity Verification**: For digital applications, the customer must complete the verification process via Nafath. For branch applications, staff must verify the physical documents.
3. **Initial Deposit**: Once the application is processed, the customer must fulfill the minimum deposit requirement to activate the account.
4. **Account Activation**: The bank will finalize the setup and activate the account within the specified timeline.

## 4. Fees and timelines
* **Minimum Deposit**: 500 SAR.
* **Activation Period**: One working day.

## 5. Required documents
To open an individual savings account, the following documents are mandatory:
* A valid National ID.
* Proof of National Address.

## 6. Notes
* **Profit Calculation**: Profits are calculated based on the daily balance.
* **Profit Distribution**: Profits are distributed on a monthly basis in accordance with the Mudarabah formula.
* **Digital Channel**: The mobile application channel is a valid method for account opening provided that verification is successfully completed through Nafath.
