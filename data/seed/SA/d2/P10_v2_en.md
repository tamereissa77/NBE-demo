# Personal finance application for employees - Version 2

Effective date: 2026-09-01

This version supersedes version 1.

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this procedure is to outline the standardized process for employees applying for personal finance facilities within the bank, ensuring consistency and compliance with credit standards.

## 2. Scope
This procedure applies to all internal employees of the bank who are eligible to apply for personal financing products under the employee benefit scheme.

## 3. Steps
1. The employee submits a formal application for personal finance through the designated internal portal.
2. The system performs an automated verification of the employee's salary and place of employment using Social Insurance (GOSI) data.
3. Upon successful automated verification, the application is referred to the Credit Committee for final review.
4. The Credit Committee reviews the application based on the established debt-to-income ratios and employment status.
5. The final decision is communicated to the employee.

## 4. Fees and timelines
* **Maximum Installment:** The monthly installment must not exceed 33.33% of the total monthly salary.
* **Maximum Duration:** The maximum repayment period is 5 years.
* **Response Time:** The turnaround time for a response is 2 working days.

## 5. Required documents
Applicants must provide only the following documents:
* National ID.
* Salary Certificate (Salary Definition).

## 6. Notes
* An additional mandatory condition for all applications is the automated verification of the salary and the employer via Social Insurance data. This verification must be completed successfully before the application can be referred to the Credit Committee.
* No other documents or fees are applicable under this specific procedure.
