# Outgoing international transfers - Version 2
Effective date: 2026-09-01

This version supersedes version 1.

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this procedure is to outline the standardized process for executing outgoing international transfers, ensuring all transactions are processed accurately and in compliance with bank regulations.

## 2. Scope
This procedure applies to all bank employees responsible for processing international fund transfers initiated by customers through either the mobile application or physical branch locations.

## 3. Steps
1. Receive the transfer request from the customer via the mobile application or at a branch counter.
2. Verify the customer's identity and account details.
3. Confirm the transfer amount and the destination currency.
4. Ensure the daily limit is not exceeded.
5. Collect the necessary documentation based on the transaction amount.
6. Process the transaction through the banking system.
7. Provide the customer with a transaction confirmation.

## 4. Fees and timelines
**Fees:**
*   **Mobile Application Transfers:** 15 SAR per transfer.
*   **Branch Transfers:** 35 SAR per transfer.

**Timelines:**
*   **Execution Period:** 2 business days.

**Daily Limit:**
*   The maximum daily limit is 100,000 SAR or its equivalent.

## 5. Required documents
To complete an international transfer, the following must be provided:
*   Transfer form.
*   Statement of purpose for the transfer.
*   **Additional Requirement:** For all transfers exceeding 20,000 SAR, a supporting document must be provided (such as an invoice or a contract).

## 6. Notes
*   All staff must ensure that the purpose of the transfer is clearly documented in the system.
*   Strict adherence to the daily limit of 100,000 SAR or its equivalent is mandatory for all transaction types.
*   The requirement for supporting documentation (invoice or contract) is strictly enforced for any amount greater than 20,000 SAR.
