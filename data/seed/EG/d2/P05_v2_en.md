# Outgoing international transfers - Version 2
Effective date: 2026-09-01

This version supersedes version 1.

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this procedure is to outline the standardized process for executing outgoing international fund transfers, ensuring all transactions are processed accurately and in compliance with bank regulations.

## 2. Scope
This procedure applies to all bank personnel responsible for processing international wire transfers requested by clients through any official banking channel.

## 3. Steps
1. Receive the transfer request from the client.
2. Verify the client's identity and account availability for the requested amount.
3. Collect all necessary documentation as specified in the requirements section.
4. Validate the purpose of the transfer to ensure it meets regulatory standards.
5. Check if the transfer amount exceeds the threshold requiring additional supporting documentation.
6. Input the transaction details into the banking system.
7. Apply the applicable commission based on the transaction value.
8. Execute the transfer and provide the client with a confirmation receipt.

## 4. Fees and timelines
* **Commission:** 0.4% with a minimum of 20 USD.
* **Execution timeline:** 2 business days.

## 5. Required documents
* Transfer form.
* Statement of the reason for the transfer.
* **Additional requirement:** For transfers exceeding 5,000 USD, a supporting document must be provided (such as an invoice or a contract).

## 6. Notes
* **Daily Limit:** The maximum daily limit for outgoing international transfers is 25,000 USD or its equivalent.
* All staff must ensure that the reason for the transfer is clearly documented to prevent processing delays.
* Ensure that the supporting documents for transfers over 5,000 USD are valid and match the transaction details.
