# Opening an individual savings account - Version 2

Effective date: 2026-09-01

This version supersedes version 1.

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this procedure is to outline the standardized process for opening an individual savings account, ensuring consistency and efficiency across all banking channels.

## 2. Scope
This procedure applies to all bank employees handling account opening requests, whether processed through physical branches or digital channels.

## 3. Steps
1. **Customer Identification:** Verify the customer's identity using the required legal documentation.
2. **Application Submission:**
    * **Branch Channel:** The customer completes the application form in person at the branch.
    * **Digital Channel:** Existing customers may open an account via the mobile application without the need to visit a branch.
3. **Document Verification:** Review the validity of the provided identification and supporting documents.
4. **Initial Deposit:** Collect the minimum required deposit to activate the account.
5. **Account Activation:** Process the application through the core banking system.

## 4. Fees and timelines
* **Minimum Deposit:** 500 EGP.
* **Activation Period:** One working day.

## 5. Required documents
To open an individual savings account, the following documents must be provided:
* A valid National ID card.
* A recent utility bill.

## 6. Notes
* **Interest Calculation:** Interest is calculated on the daily balance and is paid out on a monthly basis.
* **Digital Accessibility:** For the convenience of existing customers, the mobile application serves as a new channel, allowing for account opening without a physical branch visit.
* **Compliance:** All staff must ensure that the National ID provided is currently valid at the time of the transaction.
