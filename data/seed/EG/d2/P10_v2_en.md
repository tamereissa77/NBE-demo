# Personal loan application for employees - Version 2

Effective date: 2026-09-01

This version supersedes version 1.

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this procedure is to outline the standardized process for employees applying for personal loans, ensuring consistency, accuracy, and efficiency in credit assessment and approval.

## 2. Scope
This procedure applies to all bank employees seeking personal financing through the bank’s internal lending facilities.

## 3. Steps
1. **Application Submission**: The employee submits the formal loan application along with all required supporting documentation.
2. **Automated Verification**: The system performs an automated verification of all submitted documents. This step is mandatory and must be successfully completed before the application can be referred to the Credit Committee.
3. **Credit Committee Review**: Once automated verification is successful, the application is forwarded to the Credit Committee for final evaluation and decision-making.
4. **Notification**: The applicant is notified of the final decision regarding their loan request.

## 4. Fees and timelines
* **Response Time**: The bank will provide a response regarding the application within 5 working days.

## 5. Required documents
Applicants must provide the following documents to complete their application:
* National ID card.
* Employment letter (HR letter).
* Bank statements for the last 3 months.

## 6. Notes
* **Maximum Installment**: The monthly loan installment must not exceed 40% of the employee's net monthly income.
* **Maximum Duration**: The maximum repayment period for the personal loan is 7 years.
* **Mandatory Pre-condition**: Automated document verification is an additional requirement that must occur prior to any referral to the Credit Committee.
