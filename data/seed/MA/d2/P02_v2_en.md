# Opening an individual savings account - Version 2

Effective date: 2026-09-01

This version supersedes version 1.

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this procedure is to define the standardized process for opening an individual savings account within the bank, ensuring consistency and regulatory compliance.

## 2. Scope
This procedure applies to all bank personnel handling account openings and covers both in-branch applications and digital applications via the mobile platform.

## 3. Steps
1. **Customer Identification**: Verify the identity of the applicant using the required legal documentation.
2. **Application Submission**:
    * **In-Branch**: The customer submits a physical application at the agency.
    * **Digital Channel**: Existing customers may open an account directly through the mobile application without the need to visit a physical agency.
3. **Verification**: The bank officer or the automated system validates the provided information and documents.
4. **Initial Deposit**: The customer must provide the minimum required deposit to activate the account.
5. **Account Activation**: The system processes the request to finalize the account setup.

## 4. Fees and timelines
* **Minimum Deposit**: 200 MAD.
* **Activation Timeline**: One working day.
* **Interest Calculation**: Interest is calculated on the minimum balance every half-month and is credited to the account every three months.

## 5. Required documents
To open an individual savings account, the following documents must be provided:
* A valid National Electronic Identity Card (CNIE).
* A certificate of residence (شهادة السكنى).

## 6. Notes
* For existing customers, the mobile application serves as a new channel for account opening, eliminating the requirement for an agency visit.
* Ensure all document scans are clear and the CNIE is currently valid before proceeding with the digital or manual entry.
