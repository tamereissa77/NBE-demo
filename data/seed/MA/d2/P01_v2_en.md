# Lost card replacement - Version 2

Effective date: 2026-09-01

This version supersedes version 1.

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this procedure is to define the standardized process for replacing lost bank cards to ensure security and operational efficiency within the branch.

## 2. Scope
This procedure applies to all bank personnel responsible for handling card replacement requests for retail customers.

## 3. Steps
1. **Card Blocking:** The customer must be blocked on the lost card before the issuance of a replacement card can proceed.
2. **Identity Verification:** The agent must verify the customer's identity by checking a valid Electronic National Identity Card (CNIE).
3. **Security Authentication:** The agent must perform a secondary verification using a One-Time Password (OTP) sent to the customer's registered mobile phone.
4. **Request Processing:** Once verified, the agent initiates the replacement order in the banking system.
5. **Card Delivery:** The new card will be delivered to the customer at the agency.

## 4. Fees and timelines
The following fee structure applies to card replacements:
* **Debit Card fees:** 30 MAD
* **Credit Card fees:** 80 MAD
* **Exemption:** Salary account cards are exempt from all replacement fees.

**Delivery Timeline:**
The replacement card will be available for collection within 5 working days.

## 5. Required documents
To complete the replacement process, the following must be presented:
* A valid Electronic National Identity Card (CNIE).
* Access to the registered mobile phone to receive the OTP.

## 6. Notes
* It is mandatory to ensure the lost card is fully blocked in the system prior to any new card issuance to prevent fraudulent activity.
* All replacements are processed through the agency; home delivery is not an option under this procedure.
