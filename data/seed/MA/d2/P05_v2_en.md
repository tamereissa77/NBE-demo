# Outgoing international transfers - Version 2
Effective date: 2026-09-01

This version supersedes version 1.

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this procedure is to define the standardized process and regulatory requirements for executing outgoing international transfers from the bank's accounts, ensuring compliance with established limits and documentation standards.

## 2. Scope
This procedure applies to all bank personnel responsible for processing international wire transfers and all clients requesting to send funds outside of Morocco.

## 3. Steps
1. Receive the transfer request from the client.
2. Verify the client's available balance and ensure the request falls within the permitted annual allocation.
3. Collect all necessary documentation as specified in Section 5.
4. Validate the transfer amount against the annual limit of 150,000 MAD or its equivalent.
5. For transfers exceeding 20,000 MAD, ensure the mandatory supporting document is attached.
6. Calculate the applicable commission based on the transfer amount.
7. Execute the transfer in the banking system.
8. Confirm the transaction completion to the client.

## 4. Fees and timelines
* **Commission:** 0.4% with a minimum fee of 120 MAD.
* **Execution timeline:** 2 working days.

## 5. Required documents
To process an international transfer, the following documents must be provided:
* Transfer form.
* Statement of the reason for the transfer.
* For transfers exceeding 20,000 MAD: A supporting document (such as an invoice or a contract).

## 6. Notes
* **Annual Limit:** All outgoing international transfers are subject to an annual limit of 150,000 MAD or its equivalent within the framework of the permitted allocations.
* **Compliance:** No transfer shall be processed without the required supporting documentation for amounts exceeding the 20,000 MAD threshold.
