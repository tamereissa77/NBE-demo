# Consumer loan application for employees - Version 2

Effective date: 2026-09-01

This version supersedes version 1.

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this procedure is to define the standardized process for employees applying for consumer loans within the bank, ensuring consistency and compliance with internal credit standards.

## 2. Scope
This procedure applies to all bank employees requesting a consumer loan and governs the administrative and evaluative steps required for application processing.

## 3. Steps
1. **Document Submission**: The employee must submit the required documentation to the human resources or designated credit department.
2. **Automated Inquiry**: Before any application is referred to the Credit Committee, an automated inquiry must be conducted with the Loan Office to verify eligibility and current standing.
3. **Credit Committee Referral**: Following a successful automated inquiry, the application is formally referred to the Credit Committee for final review and decision-making.
4. **Notification**: The applicant is notified of the decision regarding their loan request.

## 4. Fees and timelines
* **Response Time**: The bank shall provide a response to the application within 5 working days.

## 5. Required documents
To ensure a complete application, the following documents are mandatory:
* National Identity Card (CNI).
* The most recent payslip only.

## 6. Notes
* **Maximum Installment**: The monthly loan repayment amount must not exceed 45% of the employee's net monthly salary.
* **Maximum Duration**: The maximum allowable term for the consumer loan is 7 years.
* **Mandatory Pre-check**: The automated inquiry at the Loan Office is a mandatory prerequisite; no application shall be presented to the Credit Committee without this step being completed.
