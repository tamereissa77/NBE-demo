# Raising daily transfer limits - Version 2

Effective date: 2026-09-01

This version supersedes version 1.

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this procedure is to outline the standardized process for customers wishing to increase their daily fund transfer limits, ensuring security and operational consistency across all banking channels.

## 2. Scope
This procedure applies to all retail banking customers requesting modifications to their daily transaction thresholds via the mobile application or through physical branch interactions.

## 3. Steps
1. **Mobile Application Request**: The customer accesses the mobile application to view current limits.
2. **Automated Limit Increase**: The customer may request an increase within the application's predefined parameters.
3. **OTP Verification**: To authorize the higher limit within the app, the customer must successfully complete verification via a One-Time Password (OTP).
4. **Branch Request**: If the customer requires a limit exceeding the maximum mobile capacity, they must visit a physical bank branch.
5. **Manual Processing**: The customer must submit a formal signed request at the branch for manual processing by the banking staff.

## 4. Fees and timelines
*   **Mobile App Limit**: The maximum limit achievable via the application is 100,000 MAD.
*   **Branch Limit Activation**: For requests exceeding the mobile maximum, the new limit becomes effective on the following business day.

## 5. Required documents
*   **For Mobile Users**: Access to the registered mobile number for OTP verification.
*   **For Branch Users**: A signed written request submitted in person at the agency.

## 6. Notes
*   **Default Limit**: The standard default limit is 30,000 MAD via the mobile application.
*   **Maximum App Limit**: The absolute maximum limit available through the application is 100,000 MAD, subject to OTP verification.
*   **Exceeding Limits**: Any amount exceeding 100,000 MAD requires a signed request at the agency and will take effect on the next business day.
