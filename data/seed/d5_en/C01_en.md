# Financing Contract No. C01

Borrower: Al-Ofok Trading Company (specimen) · Financing amount: EGP 2,500,000 · Number of instalments: 60

Specimen contract for demonstration purposes. English translation of C01.md.

## Article 1 - Parties

This contract is concluded between the National Bank of Egypt (demo specimen), hereinafter referred to as the "Lender", and Al-Ofok Trading Company (specimen), hereinafter referred to as the "Borrower". Both parties acknowledge their full legal capacity to contract, and that the information stated in the financing application forms an integral part of this contract.

## Article 2 - Financing Amount and Purpose

The Lender grants the Borrower financing in the amount of EGP 2,500,000 to be used for personal purposes, and the financing may not be used for any other purpose. The financing amount shall be credited to the Borrower's account with the Bank once all documents and securities stipulated in this contract have been provided.

## Article 3 - Interest

A variable rate of interest is charged on the outstanding financing balance, linked to the Central Bank's overnight lending rate plus a margin of 2.5% per annum. The Lender shall notify the Borrower in writing of any change in the interest rate at least fifteen days before it takes effect.

## Article 4 - Repayment

The Borrower undertakes to repay the financing and interest in 60 equal monthly instalments. Each instalment falls due on the first day of each month and is debited automatically from the Borrower's account with the Bank.

## Article 5 - Early Repayment

The Borrower may repay the financing early, in full or in part, at any time after notifying the Lender in writing. An early repayment fee of 1% of the amount repaid in advance shall be payable, and the remaining instalments shall be rescheduled or their term shortened at the Borrower's choice.

## Article 6 - Late Payment

If the Borrower is late in paying any instalment for more than 60 days from its due date, the Lender may, without the need for notice or a court judgment, declare the loan immediately due and demand the entire outstanding balance, together with a late-payment penalty of 3% per month on the overdue amounts.

## Article 7 - Insurance

Life insurance is mandatory throughout the financing term with one of the approved insurance companies. The Bank shall be the beneficiary of the insurance policy up to the outstanding balance, and any excess shall be returned to the Borrower's heirs.

## Article 8 - Securities

As security for the fulfilment of its obligations, the Borrower undertakes to transfer its salary to an account with the Bank throughout the financing term, and may not transfer its salary to another bank before full repayment except with the Lender's written consent.

## Article 9 - Termination

The Borrower may terminate the contract by giving 30 days' written notice after paying all amounts due in full. The Lender may terminate the contract in cases of material breach after giving notice, including the provision of incorrect information or cessation of payment.

## Article 10 - Limitation of Liability

The Bank shall not be liable for indirect damages that the Borrower may suffer as a result of the performance of this contract, except in cases of fraud or gross negligence, for which the Bank shall be liable in accordance with the law.

## Article 11 - Data Confidentiality

The Bank undertakes to keep the Borrower's data confidential in accordance with Law No. 194 of 2020 (the Central Bank and Banking System Law). Data shall not be shared with third parties except with the Borrower's consent or as required by law, with the exception of credit enquiries with licensed bodies.

## Article 12 - Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This contract is governed by and construed in accordance with Egyptian law, and the Economic Courts of Cairo shall have jurisdiction over any dispute arising from the performance or interpretation of this contract.
