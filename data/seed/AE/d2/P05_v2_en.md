# Outgoing international transfers - Version 2
Effective date: 2026-09-01

This version supersedes version 1.

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this procedure is to outline the standardized process for executing outgoing international fund transfers, ensuring all transactions are processed accurately and in compliance with bank regulations.

## 2. Scope
This procedure applies to all bank employees responsible for processing international wire transfers initiated by customers through either the mobile application or physical branch locations.

## 3. Steps
1. Receive the transfer request from the customer via the mobile application or at a branch counter.
2. Verify the customer's identity and account details.
3. Confirm the recipient's international banking information (IBAN/SWIFT).
4. Validate that the requested amount does not exceed the daily limit of AED 100,000 or its equivalent.
5. Check for the presence of required documentation based on the transaction value.
6. Process the transaction through the core banking system.
7. Issue a confirmation to the customer.

## 4. Fees and timelines
The following fee structure and processing times apply to all outgoing international transfers:

*   **Transaction Fees:**
    *   Transfers via the mobile application: AED 25
    *   Transfers via a branch: AED 60
*   **Execution Timeline:**
    *   Processing time: 2 working days

## 5. Required documents
To ensure successful processing, the following documentation must be provided:
*   Transfer form.
*   Statement of purpose for the transfer.
*   **Additional Requirement:** For all transfers exceeding AED 35,000, a supporting document must be provided (such as an invoice or a contract).

## 6. Notes
*   The daily limit of AED 100,000 or its equivalent is strictly enforced for all international outgoing transfers.
*   Failure to provide the necessary supporting documents for transactions over AED 35,000 will result in the delay or rejection of the transfer request.
