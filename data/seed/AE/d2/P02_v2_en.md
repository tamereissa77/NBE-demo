# Opening an individual savings account - Version 2
Effective date: 2026-09-01

This version supersedes version 1.

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this procedure is to outline the standardized process for opening an individual savings account, ensuring compliance with banking regulations and providing clear guidance for staff and customers.

## 2. Scope
This procedure applies to all individual customers seeking to open a savings account through either physical branch locations or digital channels.

## 3. Steps
1. **Application Submission**: The customer may initiate the application through two primary methods:
    * **Branch Visit**: The customer visits a physical branch to complete the application in person.
    * **Mobile Application**: The customer utilizes the bank's mobile application to open the account remotely.
2. **Identity Verification**: 
    * For branch applications, staff must verify physical documents.
    * For mobile applications, the customer must complete the verification process via UAE PASS. This allows for account opening without the requirement of a branch visit.
3. **Documentation Review**: The bank officer or the digital system must verify all submitted identification and residency documents.
4. **Account Activation**: Once the application is approved and verified, the account undergoes an activation process.

## 4. Fees and timelines
* **Minimum Balance**: A minimum balance of 1,000 AED is required to maintain the account.
* **Activation Timeline**: The account activation period is one working day.

## 5. Required documents
To successfully open an individual savings account, the following documents must be provided:
* Emirates ID
* Passport
* Proof of residence

## 6. Notes
* **Interest Calculation**: Interest is calculated on the daily balance and is disbursed on a monthly basis.
* **Digital Channel**: The introduction of the mobile application and UAE PASS integration provides a new channel for customers to manage their banking needs efficiently without visiting a branch.
