# Personal loan application for employees - Version 2

Effective date: 2026-09-01

This version supersedes version 1.

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this procedure is to outline the standardized process for employees applying for personal loans within the bank, ensuring consistency and compliance with credit policies.

## 2. Scope
This procedure applies to all bank employees seeking personal financing under the employee loan scheme.

## 3. Steps
1. **Document Submission**: The employee must submit the required documentation to the Human Resources or Credit Department.
2. **Automated Credit Check**: Before the application is referred to the Credit Committee, an automated inquiry must be conducted through the Al Etihad Credit Bureau (AECB) to assess the applicant's creditworthiness.
3. **Credit Committee Referral**: Following a successful automated credit check, the application is formally referred to the Credit Committee for final review and approval.
4. **Notification**: The applicant will be notified of the decision regarding their application.

## 4. Fees and timelines
* **Response Time**: The bank will provide a response regarding the application status within two (2) working days.

## 5. Required documents
To process the application, the following documents are mandatory:
* Emirates ID
* Salary Certificate

## 6. Notes
* **Maximum Installment**: The total monthly installment for all financial obligations must not exceed 50% of the employee's total monthly salary.
* **Maximum Tenure**: The maximum repayment period for the personal loan is 48 months.
* **Credit Inquiry Requirement**: An automated inquiry from the Al Etihad Credit Bureau is a mandatory prerequisite before any application can be referred to the Credit Committee.
