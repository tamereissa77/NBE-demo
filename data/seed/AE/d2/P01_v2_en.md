# Lost card replacement - Version 2
Effective date: 2026-09-01

This version supersedes version 1.

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this procedure is to outline the standardized process for replacing lost bank cards to ensure security and operational efficiency within the bank.

## 2. Scope
This procedure applies to all bank employees responsible for processing card replacement requests for both debit and credit cardholders.

## 3. Steps
1. **Card Deactivation:** Before any replacement can be issued, the customer must ensure the lost card is officially blocked. This must be completed via the mobile application or by contacting the Call Center.
2. **Identity Verification:** The employee must verify that the customer’s Emirates ID is valid. Furthermore, identity verification must be completed through UAE PASS.
3. **Request Processing:** Once identity is confirmed and the lost card is deactivated, the employee shall initiate the replacement request in the system.
4. **Card Issuance:** The system will process the order for the new card based on the customer's card type.

## 4. Fees and timelines
The following fees apply to card replacement:
* **Debit Card Fee:** 25 AED
* **Credit Card Fee:** 75 AED
* **Exemption:** Salary account cards are exempt from all replacement fees.

**Delivery Timeline:**
* The replacement card will be available for collection within 3 working days.
* **Delivery Location:** The card must be collected from the branch.

## 5. Required documents
To complete the replacement process, the following are mandatory:
* A valid Emirates ID.
* Successful authentication via UAE PASS.

## 6. Notes
* It is a mandatory condition that the lost card is stopped/blocked through the application or the Call Center prior to the issuance of any replacement card.
* Ensure all fee exemptions for salary accounts are applied correctly during the transaction.
